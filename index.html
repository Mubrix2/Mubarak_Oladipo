Business problem

As the company grew past a handful of clients, the founder was still running day-to-day operations manually — replying to emails between meetings, tracking tasks in a personal notebook, and coordinating calendars over text. There was no shared system the assistant or operations team could step into, so every recurring task depended on the founder remembering it themselves.
