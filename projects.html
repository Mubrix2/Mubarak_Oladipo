Client Onboarding & Data Routing Ecosystem

Self-Directed Project  ·  Client Onboarding & Executive Operations

The Business Problem

Manual client onboarding caused communication bottlenecks, data fragmentation across disjointed tools, and a lack of real-time visibility into onboarding pipelines.

Solution (Action Taken)

Engineered a multi-stage automated onboarding pipeline using Make.com to orchestrate workflows across Zoho Invoice, Google Workspace (Drive, Forms, Sheets, Gmail), Gemini AI, and Slack. Designed a human-in-the-loop QA checkpoint in Slack for internal verification before triggering AI-generated briefs and automated client folders.

System snapshots
Operational Impact

  • EA-Driven Risk Visibility

    Gave the EA a live operational dashboard showing every active client's stage and days-in-onboarding, enabling proactive follow-up decisions — routine cases handled directly, higher-value or higher-risk situations flagged for executive attention — without requiring the EA to manually compile status across scattered tools.

  • Improved Operational Consistency

    Standardized recurring administrative processes through documented SOPs, reducing dependence on memory and enabling repeatable execution.

  • Better Collaboration

    Established organized communication channels, shared documentation, and collaborative workspaces that support effective coordination across team members.

Automated Accounts Receivable & Reporting Dashboard

Operations Support  ·  Finance Administration  ·  Executive Reporting

The Business Problem

Disconnected invoice tracking led to poor cash-flow visibility, delayed payments, and time-consuming manual tracking of overdue client accounts.

Solution (Action Taken)

Built an automated revenue operations system linking Zoho Invoice to Google Sheets via Make.com, creating a live executive KPI dashboard. Configured Gemini 2.5 Flash to auto-draft context-aware, tone-escalating payment reminders, routing them through Gmail only after manual approval.

System snapshots
Business impact

  • Centralized Executive Operations

    Consolidated executive scheduling, communication, documentation, and task management into a single operational system, making information easier to locate and manage.

  • Improved Operational Consistency

    Standardized recurring administrative processes through documented SOPs, reducing dependence on memory and enabling repeatable execution.

  • Better Collaboration

    Established organized communication channels, shared documentation, and collaborative workspaces that support effective coordination across team members.

Lead Ingestion & Response Time (SLA) Workflow

Administrative Operations  ·  Client Communication  ·  Workflow Coordination

The Business Problem

Slow response times to incoming customer and client inquiries resulted in dropped leads and a lack of data on internal communication performance.

Solution (Action Taken)

Designed an end-to-end inbound communication pipeline using Zapier and Zapier Tables as a lightweight operational CRM. Integrated OpenAI to auto-draft responses directly into a Slack approval channel, streamlining the feedback loop before sending via Gmail.

System snapshots
Business impact

  • Centralized Financial Tracking

    Structured invoicing and reporting processes for consistent financial record-keeping.

  • Revenue Visibility

    Interactive dashboards showing revenue, outstanding balances, and payment status in real time.

  • Standardized Meeting Operations

    Reusable templates and action trackers for meeting preparation and follow-up.

  • Documented Procedures

    Established operational SOPs ensuring consistent execution and smoother onboarding.
