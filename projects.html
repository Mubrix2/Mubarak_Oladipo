Paid Media Spend Optimization & Budget Decision System

Self-Directed Project  ·  Marketing Operations  ·  Data Analysis  ·  Budget Decision Support

The Business Problem

Advertising performance was distributed across Google Ads and Meta Ads, making it difficult to identify which campaigns were generating efficient returns and where budget could potentially be reallocated while protecting conversion volume.

Solution (Action Taken)

Built a structured paid media optimization workflow using Google Sheets to standardize campaign data, calculate CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS and conversion metrics, evaluate campaign performance by platform, apply sample-size checks, and develop a budget redistribution scenario. Used AI as a decision-support layer to identify campaigns for increased, reduced, or paused investment, while retaining human review before any budget changes.

Client Onboarding & Data Routing Ecosystem

Self-Directed Project  ·  Business Operations & Client Onboarding

The Business Problem

Manual client onboarding caused communication bottlenecks, data fragmentation across disjointed tools, and a lack of real-time visibility into onboarding pipelines.

Solution (Action Taken)

Engineered a multi-stage automated onboarding pipeline using Make.com to orchestrate workflows across Zoho Invoice, Google Workspace (Drive, Forms, Sheets, Gmail), Gemini AI, and Slack. Designed a human-in-the-loop QA checkpoint in Slack for internal verification before triggering AI-generated briefs and automated client folders.

Automated Accounts Receivable & Reporting Dashboard

Operations Support  ·  Finance Administration  ·  Executive Reporting

The Business Problem

Disconnected invoice tracking led to poor cash-flow visibility, delayed payments, and time-consuming manual tracking of overdue client accounts.

Solution (Action Taken)

Built an automated revenue operations system linking Zoho Invoice to Google Sheets via Make.com, creating a live executive KPI dashboard. Configured Gemini 2.5 Flash to auto-draft context-aware, tone-escalating payment reminders, routing them through Gmail only after manual approval.

Lead Ingestion & Response Time (SLA) Workflow

Operations Support  ·  Client Communication  ·  Process Coordination

The Business Problem

Slow response times to incoming customer and client inquiries resulted in dropped leads and a lack of data on internal communication performance.

Solution (Action Taken)

Designed an end-to-end inbound communication pipeline using Zapier and Zapier Tables as a lightweight operational CRM. Integrated OpenAI to auto-draft responses directly into a Slack approval channel, streamlining the feedback loop before sending via Gmail.
